# Style DNA
- Premium contemporary travel editorial meets impressionist oil painting; painterly realism, not a photo filter.
- Directional impasto and palette-knife marks, layered pigment, varied stroke sizes: broader sky strokes, finer architectural and figure details.
- Luminous warm sunlight, cream highlights, cool blue/violet shadows; vivid but harmonious scene-derived colors. Do not force daylight on an intentionally nocturnal scene.
- Warm ivory upper negative space (approx #F8EFDF), charcoal handwritten subtitle (#50534D), sky blue where appropriate (#559CE6). These are inferred targets, not sampled mandatory colors.
- Keep the original setting and emotional narrative. Natural vertical outpainting is preferred to stretching or destructive cropping.
- No flat vectors, cartoon, anime, watercolor washes, plastic 3D, excessive HDR, harsh black shadows, logos, borders or mockup frames.
- Full bleed at bottom and sides of the landscape, with deliberate cream space above. Subtle paper character, not heavy noise.
